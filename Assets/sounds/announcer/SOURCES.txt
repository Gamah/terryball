Terryball announcer voice-overs.

Recorded by the project owner (Gamah) in a single take session, self-performed.
License: project-owned / public domain for use in Terryball. No third-party rights.

Source recording: one continuous ~67s take, each call performed TWICE in a row
("take 0" and "take 1" — used for playback variety). Auto-sliced on the silence
gaps between calls, then per-clip processed.

Processing:
  dry/  -> highpass 85Hz + loudnorm (-16 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP). Clean, no ambience.
  fx/   -> highpass 70Hz, dominant dry voice + a quiet MONO convolution room
          reverb (decaying-noise impulse, mixed ~well below the voice so it sits
          behind it), low-shelf boom (+6dB @95Hz), centered (dual-mono) output,
          loudnorm, trailing-tail trim, safety limiter.
          Mono/centered on purpose: earlier detune and decorrelated-stereo-reverb
          attempts caused phasing and hard-left panning. No pitch/detune at all.
          Use these for the in-game announcer feel; swap to dry/ if too wet.

_review_fx.wav / _review_dry.wav: all 25 calls (take 0) concatenated in script
order, for quick listening. Not used by the game; delete before shipping if desired.

Calls (script order): 1-9, strike, spare, gutter, miss, open-frame, split,
double, turkey, four-bagger, five-bagger, six-pack, nice, so-close, good-try,
pocket (in the pocket), perfect-game.
